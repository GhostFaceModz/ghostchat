Features

Everything a premium assistant needs

Built for creators, shops and SaaS teams who want answers that are actually right, in a widget that looks like it belongs on their site.

Train

Learns your content, not the whole internet

Point GhostChat at your site and it crawls your pages and sitemap. Add PDFs, docs, a pasted FAQ or a Notion page. If the answer isn't in your content, it says so instead of making things up.

  • Site crawl with sitemap.xml support
  • PDF, Markdown, HTML, CSV and text uploads
  • Source links and follow-up suggestions on answers
Brand

Looks and sounds like you

Pick a template (support, sales or docs), set the tone and greeting, and choose your colour and light or dark mode. The glass chat panel and animated ghost launcher feel premium on desktop and mobile.

  • Persona, tone, starter questions and guardrails
  • Custom colour, dark or light theme, optional sound and voice
  • Remove the "Powered by" badge on Pro and Business
Convert

Turns questions into customers

Show product, discount and booking cards right inside the chat when visitors ask about them. Capture emails with the transcript attached, and see what people ask most in analytics.

  • Cal.com and Calendly booking links
  • Product, discount code and lead-form cards
  • Transcripts, leads and analytics in your dashboard
🔗

Shareable chat link

No website yet? Share a hosted chat page or print its QR code.

🛡️

Guardrails built in

Banned topics, personal-data redaction and rate limits keep things safe.

🧠

Pick your model

Choose from the AI providers your server has keys for, with automatic fallback.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need to code?

No. You copy one line from your dashboard and paste it into your site's HTML, or into the "custom code" box on Shopify, Wix, Squarespace, WordPress or Webflow.

Where does it get its answers?

Only from the content you give it: your crawled pages, uploaded files and pasted notes. Answers can link back to the page they came from.

What happens when it doesn't know?

It tells the visitor it isn't sure and can offer to take their email so you can follow up, so nobody gets a made-up answer.

How do I upgrade?

Open Plan & billing in your dashboard and check out with Whop using the same email as your account. Your new limits apply automatically.
