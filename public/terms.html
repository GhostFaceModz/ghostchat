Terms

Plain-English rules for using GhostChat.

The service

GhostChat lets you build an AI assistant trained on your own content and add it to your website. It is provided as it is, and we can't promise it will always be available or error-free.

Your content

  • You keep ownership of what you add. You let GhostChat store and process it to run your assistant.
  • Only add content you have the right to use, and don't train it on other people's private data.

AI answers

Replies are generated by AI from your content and can be wrong. Check important answers, and don't rely on the assistant for legal, medical or financial advice.

Fair use

  • No spam, abuse, illegal content, malware or attempts to break or overload the service.
  • Don't use GhostChat to collect personal data without telling visitors.
  • We may limit or suspend accounts that break these rules.

Plans and billing

The Free plan has usage limits. Paid plans are billed through Whop, and Whop's checkout terms apply to payments. You can cancel through Whop at any time.

Changes and contact

We may update these terms and will change the date above when we do. Questions: GitHub issues. See also our Privacy page.
